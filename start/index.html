Common questions

Good to know.

Do I need to download an app?

No. ZeroHours Spend runs in your browser and can be added to a supported phone's home screen.

Can I scan a receipt instead of typing everything?

Yes. Upload a supported receipt image or PDF and review the AI-extracted details before saving. Scanning requires an internet connection.

Can I share expenses with someone else?

Yes. Shared access is available with permission controls. Shared workspaces require an internet connection.

Can I search individual products?

Yes. ZeroHours Spend can save receipt line items, which you can search by product name. You can also filter expense records by category, date and amount.

Are spending alerts automatic?

In-app spending alerts are evaluated when you use the app. Device push notifications and automatic email alerts are not currently available.

Can I export my records?

Yes. Export expense data to a CSV file that can be opened in spreadsheet software.

Will it prepare my tax return?

No. ZeroHours Spend helps organize expense records; it is not tax filing software or a substitute for professional tax advice.
